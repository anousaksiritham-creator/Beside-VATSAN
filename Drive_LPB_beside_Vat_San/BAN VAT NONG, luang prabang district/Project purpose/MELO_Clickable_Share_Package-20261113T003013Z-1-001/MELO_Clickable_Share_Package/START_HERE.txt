HOW TO OPEN
1. Download and unzip this ZIP file.
2. Open MELO_Clickable_Proposal.html in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
   Open it in the browser itself, rather than a messaging app's file preview.
3. Use the arrow buttons or the slide menu to move through all 30 slides.

EVERYTHING IS INCLUDED
All presentation images and fonts are embedded in the HTML file.
No installation or internet connection is required after downloading.

EXTRA VIEWS

Floor workshop: move staff markers and download discussion notes.
Ingredients: shared ingredient system.
Full screen: presentation mode.

A laptop or desktop is best for viewing the slides and floor workshop.
Workshop changes are temporary. Download notes before closing the file.
